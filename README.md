Task 4: Interactive Business Intelligence Dashboard
Project Objective
Designed and developed an interactive sales performance dashboard using the Superstore Sales dataset to provide business stakeholders with key performance insights, regional analysis, and historical sales trend visualization.
Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Total Sales: $2.29M
Total Profit: $286.39K
Profit Margin: 12.47%
Dashboard Features
1. Executive Summary Cards
Displays high-level business metrics, including Total Sales, Total Profit, and Profit Margin, enabling quick assessment of overall business performance.
2. Dynamic Regional Filtering
Implemented an interactive region selector that allows users to analyze performance across different regions. For example:
East Region Sales: $678,781.24
East Region Profit: $91,522.78
3. Sales Trend Analysis
Created a time-series line chart covering the period from 2014 to 2017 to track sales growth and business performance over time. The analysis highlights:
A brief slowdown in 2015
Consistent recovery and growth afterward
Strong upward momentum through 2017
